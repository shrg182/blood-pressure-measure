Important limitations

The app records the external meter’s value; the phone camera does not measure SpO₂. Movement, poor circulation, cold fingers, nail polish, skin pigmentation, sensor placement, and the meter itself can affect accuracy. Follow your clinician’s action plan and do not rely on one reading when symptoms are severe.
