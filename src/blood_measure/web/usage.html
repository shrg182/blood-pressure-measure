Usage instructions

For a steadier camera pulse recording, prepare first and keep still.

Before measuring

  1. Sit comfortably and rest for a few minutes.
  2. Use a supported browser over HTTPS and allow rear-camera access.
  3. Warm cold hands and remove any phone case that blocks the camera or flash.

During the recording

  1. Cover the rear camera and flash completely with your fingertip.
  2. Use gentle, steady pressure—do not press hard.
  3. Keep your hand and phone still until the 15-second recording finishes.
  4. If signal quality stays poor, reposition your finger and try again.

Using a cuff reference

Immediately after recording, take a reading with a validated upper-arm cuff and save its pressure and pulse. After three usable pairs, the app applies bounded personal adjustments to comparison values.

Important limitation

A phone camera detects a pulse waveform and cannot directly measure blood pressure. Personal adjustment only changes the displayed estimate and does not make it a medical measurement. Confirm every health decision or unusual reading with a validated upper-arm cuff or a clinician.

Privacy and saved readings

Paired readings and preferences stay in this browser. Export readings before clearing browser data if you want to keep a copy.
